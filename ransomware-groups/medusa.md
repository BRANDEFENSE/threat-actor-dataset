# Medusa

Medusa is a ransomware-as-a-service operation first identified in June 2021, according to the FBI, CISA and HHS. As of April 2026 they count over 500 victims, with healthcare a frequent target. It steals data and encrypts files, then threatens to publish the data.

|---|---|
| Status | active |
| First seen | 2021 |
| Model | Ransomware as a service (closed group until at least early 2023) |
