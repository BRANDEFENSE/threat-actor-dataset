# Akira

Akira is a ransomware group active since March 2023. A joint advisory (AA24-109A, first published April 18, 2024, revised 13 November 2025) from the FBI, CISA, Europol and agencies in France, Germany and the Netherlands says it has hit businesses and critical infrastructure in North America, Europe and Australia. As of late September 2025 it had claimed about 244.17 million US dollars in proceeds, per the advisory.
