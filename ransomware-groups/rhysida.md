# Rhysida

Rhysida is a ransomware operation that CISA, the FBI and the MS-ISAC say has mainly been deployed against education, healthcare, manufacturing, IT and government organizations since May 2023. Open source reporting describes it as a ransomware-as-a-service model where any ransom is split with associates. Rhysida uses double extortion: victims pay to decrypt files and pay again to stop stolen data being published.
