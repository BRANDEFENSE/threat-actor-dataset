# Everest

Everest is a financially motivated ransomware and data extortion group. The US Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) says it has been active since 2020, steals and threatens to publish data, runs ransomware, and also acts as an initial access broker, meaning it sells entry into company networks to other criminals.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Also known as | Everest ransomware group, EverBe 2.0 (earlier family, per HHS HC3) |
| Status | active |
| First seen | 2020 |
| Model | Extortion group with ransomware and initial access broker activity |
| Targets | Financial services, Healthcare, Government, Construction and engineering, Legal and professional services |
