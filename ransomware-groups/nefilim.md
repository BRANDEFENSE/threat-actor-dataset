# Nefilim Ransomware: Profile, Tactics and the Stryzhak Case

Nefilim was a ransomware-as-a-service operation that stole data, encrypted networks and threatened to publish what it took. The US Department of Justice says its administrators gave affiliates access in return for a percentage of ransom payments; affiliate Artem Stryzhak agreed to pay 20%. On 27 October 2026 a US court sentenced him to 70 months in prison.
