# The Gentlemen

The Gentlemen is a ransomware-as-a-service operation. Sophos says the scheme started in mid-2025 and posted its first victims to a leak site in September 2025. Unit 42 cites an outside count of 580 claimed victims in 77 countries through 7 July 2026. Check Point says entry is almost always through an unpatched internet-facing device such as a VPN or firewall.
