# Vice Society

Vice Society is an intrusion, exfiltration and extortion group that first appeared in summer 2021, per joint advisory AA22-249A (FBI, CISA and MS-ISAC, September 6, 2022). The advisory says its actors disproportionately targeted the education sector and used other groups' ransomware rather than their own.
