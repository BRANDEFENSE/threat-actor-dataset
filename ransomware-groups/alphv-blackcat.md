# ALPHV / BlackCat

ALPHV Blackcat is a ransomware as a service operation. The FBI, CISA and the US Department of Health and Human Services describe it in a joint advisory (AA23-353A, first issued December 19, 2023, revised 27 February 2024). Operators run the ransomware and affiliates carry out the attacks.
