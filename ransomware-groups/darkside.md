# DarkSide

DarkSide was a ransomware as a service group that CISA and the FBI named in advisory AA21-131A, first published May 11, 2021, after an attack on a U.S. pipeline company's IT network. A later advisory (AA21-291A, October 18, 2021) says it was active from September 2020 through May 2021 and that BlackMatter is a possible rebrand.
