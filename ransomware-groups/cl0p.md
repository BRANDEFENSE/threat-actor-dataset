# Cl0p

Cl0p is a ransomware operation first seen in February 2019 and, per a joint FBI and CISA advisory (AA23-158A, June 2023), also known as TA505. It is best known for stealing data from many organizations at once by exploiting flaws in file transfer software, including Accellion, GoAnywhere and MOVEit.
