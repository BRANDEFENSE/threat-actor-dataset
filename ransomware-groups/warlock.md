# Warlock

Warlock is a ransomware family that Microsoft says a China-based actor it tracks as Storm-2603 deployed in July 2025 after exploiting on-premises SharePoint servers. CISA's alert on the same flaws says actors were seen encrypting files and distributing Warlock on compromised systems.
