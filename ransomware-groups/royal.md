# Royal

Royal is a ransomware group that, according to a joint FBI and CISA advisory (AA23-061A, first published March 2, 2023), rebranded as BlackSuit. The advisory says BlackSuit is the evolution of Royal and shares numerous coding similarities with it. In August 2025 the US Justice Department announced the takedown of BlackSuit (Royal) servers and domains.
