# Hive

Hive was a ransomware as a service operation active from June 2021. A joint advisory (AA22-321A) from the FBI, CISA and HHS says it hit over 1,300 companies and received about 100 million US dollars in ransom. The UK National Crime Agency reports its servers were taken offline in an FBI and German-led operation on 26 January 2023.
