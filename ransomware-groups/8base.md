# 8Base

8Base is the name of a ransomware affiliate group that used the Phobos ransomware. In February 2025 the US Justice Department said it had charged two Russian nationals who allegedly ran it, and that it had hit more than 1,000 public and private entities and received over 16 million US dollars in ransom payments. The department also said Europol and German authorities announced an operation against the network's servers.
