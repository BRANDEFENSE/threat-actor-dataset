# Play

Play, also called Playcrypt, is a ransomware group active since June 2022. A joint advisory (AA23-352A, December 18, 2023, revised 4 June 2025) from the FBI, CISA and the Australian Cyber Security Centre says it was among the most active groups in 2024 and that, as of May 2025, the FBI knew of about 900 affected entities.
