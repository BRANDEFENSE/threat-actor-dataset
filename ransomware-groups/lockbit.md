# LockBit

LockBit is a ransomware as a service operation first seen in January 2020. A joint advisory (AA23-165A, June 14, 2023) calls it the most deployed ransomware variant in the world in 2022. The UK National Crime Agency took control of its services in February 2024 and, in May 2024, assessed the group as running at limited capacity.
