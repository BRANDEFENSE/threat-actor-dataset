# DearCry

DearCry is ransomware that CISA said, in a March 12, 2021 update, was being used on compromised on-premises Exchange servers. The servers had been broken into through the Exchange flaws disclosed in March 2021. The sources opened do not name who operated it.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| First seen | March 2021 |
| Model | Ransomware family deployed after Exchange server compromise |
