# Interlock

Interlock is a ransomware group first observed in late September 2024. A joint advisory (AA25-203A, released July 22, 2025) from the FBI, CISA, HHS and MS-ISAC says it has hit businesses and critical infrastructure in North America and Europe, and that it often gets in through fake browser updates on compromised websites.
