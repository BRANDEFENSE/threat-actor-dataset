# Qilin

Qilin, also tracked as Agenda, is a ransomware as a service family that MITRE ATT&CK says has been active since at least 2022. It has Go and Rust versions that target Windows, Linux and VMware ESXi. Ransomware as a service means the core team rents its malware to partner criminals, called affiliates.
