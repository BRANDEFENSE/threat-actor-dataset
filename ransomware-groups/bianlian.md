# BianLian

BianLian is a ransomware and data extortion group that the FBI, CISA and Australia's ACSC say is likely based in Russia. It has hit US and Australian organizations since June 2022 and, by about January 2024, had dropped encryption for pure data theft.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | active |
| First seen | 2022 |
| Model | Developer and deployer group with Russia-based affiliates |
| Targets | Critical infrastructure (US and Australia), Professional services, Property development |
