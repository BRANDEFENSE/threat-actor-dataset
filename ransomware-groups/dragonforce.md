# DragonForce

DragonForce is a ransomware family that, per a July 29, 2025 update to a joint advisory from agencies including CISA, the FBI and the UK's NCSC, Scattered Spider actors have most recently been deploying. The update is attributed to trusted third parties. The UK's NCSC also names the DragonForce group in its 2025 annual review, in connection with attacks on UK retail.
