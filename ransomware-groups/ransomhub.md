# RansomHub

RansomHub is a ransomware as a service operation, formerly known as Cyclops and Knight, according to a joint advisory (AA24-242A) from the FBI, CISA, MS-ISAC and HHS released on August 29, 2024. The advisory says it had encrypted and stolen data from at least 210 victims since its start in February 2024. Ransomware as a service means the core team rents its malware to partner criminals, called affiliates.
