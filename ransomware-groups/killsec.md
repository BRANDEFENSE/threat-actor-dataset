# KillSec

KillSec is a ransomware and data extortion group that Europol says has been active since around 2024. On 30 September 2026 law enforcement took control of its leak site, securing at least 110 terabytes of data, in an operation led by German authorities.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | disrupted |
| First seen | 2024 |
| Model | Data theft and extortion by a group whose suspected members held administrator, developer, negotiator and affiliate roles (attributed by Europol) |
