# TA505

CISA and the FBI say, "according to open source information", that the CL0P ransomware gang is also known as TA505, and MITRE ATT&CK describes TA505 as a cyber criminal group active since at least 2014. As attributed by those bodies, it steals data and runs ransomware, including through zero-day flaws in file transfer software such as MOVEit Transfer.
